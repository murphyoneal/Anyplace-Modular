The geometry that changes everything.

The Anyplace keying-rib joining system distributes structural load in shear across every connection. That's not a marketing claim — it's the patent. US9598852B2, NZ722998, AU2015201461B2. The same geometry that makes it strong makes it disassemble cleanly, with 98% material recovery at end of life.

The floor and ceiling frames are identical. Wall studs are removable. The superstructure is permanent. The interior is yours to change at any time, at any stage of life, without structural work and without a permit.

The interior — Payload

The interior that never goes to landfill.

The Payload system is the modular interior that fits inside the Anyplace frame. Height-adjustable. Resaleable. Repurposed as construction fencing or concrete forms at end of use. Financed separately — never at mortgage rates.

A conventional kitchen renovation costs $5,000 — but financed on a 25-year mortgage at today's rates, the true cost is $10,900. That kitchen has a 10–15 year functional life. Then it goes to landfill, and you finance it again.

Payload changes the math entirely. The cabinet system is modular, moveable, and resaleable. Kitchen counter height adjusts from standard to wheelchair-accessible in under an hour, at zero structural cost, with no permit. When the module changes hands, the interior goes with it — or sells separately. When it reaches the end of its useful life, it becomes construction site fencing, then aluminum recovery. Three value captures from one manufactured component.
